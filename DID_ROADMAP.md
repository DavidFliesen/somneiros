# Somneiros DID Expansion Roadmap

## Objective
Build an evidence-informed Dream Interpretation Database that offers multiple perspectives, personal reflection prompts, explicit uncertainty, and inspectable sources.

## Acceptance rules
- Every externally sourced claim has a real, reviewed source.
- Cultural claims identify a specific people, text, author, place, and period.
- Disputed anecdotes are labeled as disputed.
- Historical theories are not presented as modern scientific consensus.
- Predictions, diagnoses, supernatural certainty, and recovered-memory claims are excluded.
- At least one alternative interpretation or caveat is provided.
- Each record has a review date and version.

## Next targets
100 symbols, 50 actions, 40 settings, 30 recurring situations, 25 emotions, 20 documented historical dream accounts, 10 historical traditions, and 10 contemporary dream-science topics.
